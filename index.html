Three different counts. Migrant stock = born in Africa, living elsewhere. Historic diaspora = descendants of earlier migrations (≈200M people of African descent in the Americas). Mixed ancestry = anyone with some African ancestry through genealogy and intermarriage — larger still, but not measured by any global census. These are deliberately not summed.
